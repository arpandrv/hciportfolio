Shared Responsibilities

Although each team member had a primary role, the project work was collaborative. Team members contributed across stages rather than working in completely separate areas.

Shared responsibilities included:

  1. Defining the project topic and narrowing the design problem.
  2. Preparing ethics and consent materials.
  3. Recruiting CDU student participants.
  4. Conducting or supporting interviews, observations, and prototype feedback sessions.
  5. Reviewing transcripts, notes, and participant feedback.
  6. Discussing themes, user needs, and design requirements.
  7. Generating and comparing design ideas.
  8. Reviewing prototype iterations and deciding what to change.
  9. Preparing portfolio artefacts.
  10. Preparing the final report and presentation.

Communication and Coordination

The team coordinated through regular discussion, in-person meetings, and review of each project stage. Major decisions were discussed as a team, especially:

  • the final problem framing,
  • the choice of Canopy Pause as the main concept,
  • the decision to focus on study-safe nature reset breaks,
  • the inclusion of Study Handoff and return task anchor,
  • the use of comfort-aware outdoor and indoor reset options,
  • the use of Wizard-of-Oz simulation for comfort data,
  • the final prototype and evaluation direction.

Role Reflection

The team roles helped divide responsibility while keeping the project coherent. Research activities informed problem definition, problem definition informed design decisions, and evaluation findings informed the final concept. The team aimed to ensure that the final portfolio, report, and presentation were consistent with each other and supported by the same evidence.
